About Me

Production-Minded AI Automation Developer

Connecting enterprise tools with dependable, error-resilient agentic workflows.

I am an AI Automation and Agentic AI developer who builds n8n workflows and LLM-powered agents that connect directly to everyday business tools through REST APIs, webhooks, Gmail, Google Sheets, and WhatsApp. I deliver freelance automations that eliminate time-consuming manual operations and keep business workflows running smoothly.

My work centers on production reliability: every pipeline is engineered with strict validation steps, structured error handling, retries, and fallback paths. Bilingual in Arabic and English, I am currently completing the 120-hour Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) in AI & Automation while pursuing my Computer Science degree at Beni-Suef University. I approach every project with an engineering mindset built for long-term dependability.
